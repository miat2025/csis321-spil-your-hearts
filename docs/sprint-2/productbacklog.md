## Briefly explain how your team determined the priorities and Story Points:

US-02: We determined the priority on this as medium because this is the key feature that will let George Fox students fill out a form to participate in chapel. The reason why we chose 2 as the story points is because it will depend on US-01 also because it might take a day or two to get it up and correct.

US-03: We determined the priority on this as high because this is the key feature that will let George Fox students know the general list of chapel dates and times. The reason why we chose 1 as the story points is because it will depend on US-01 also because we won't have to do that much to get it up.

US-04: We determined the priority on this as medium because this is the key feature that lets SpiL faculty connect the chapel topics to the website to see what's happening in each chapel. The reason why we chose 3 as the story points is because we know some of the things that are needed to execute this as well as there is a dependence on us to connect the information at first will take a few days to do.
